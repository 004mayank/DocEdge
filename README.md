# DocEdge - AI Clinical Copilot

## Overview

**DocEdge** is an AI-powered clinical copilot that helps doctors **capture, understand, and act on patient data in real time**.

Instead of switching between notes, reports, and memory, doctors simply **talk during consultations**, and DocEdge automatically:

- Generates structured clinical notes  
- Extracts insights from reports and scans  
- Suggests possible diagnoses and next steps  
- Creates patient-ready summaries  

---

## One-liner

> **DocEdge turns conversations + medical data into actionable clinical intelligence**

---

## Why DocEdge

Healthcare workflows today are fragmented:

- Conversations are not recorded  
- Reports are scattered (paper, WhatsApp, PDFs)  
- Doctors rely on memory  
- Documentation is time-consuming
